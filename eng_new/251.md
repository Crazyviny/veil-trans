When you Probe someone, you may always ask, “What is your character
really feeling?” For free.
Assuming you get a hit and can ask a question when Probing someone,
you may then also ask this question without it counting toward the number
of questions in total you get to ask, determined by your roll. From finding
out if someone is being deceitful, or lying, to being able to comfort a fellow
protagonist; the additional option always for you to get free information to
springboard off of when deciding on the next question to pose..
