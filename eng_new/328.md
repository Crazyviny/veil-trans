THE EXECUTIVE
The Executive, at first glance, is very powerful. However, the first thing
you see about them is that they are instantly on a leash. Holding it, is the
board. They further their own agenda by way of the protagonist. This is a
great opportunity for the MC to incorporate the Final Question you want
to incorporate for your campaign. The board could be manipulating them
or a higher power is pulling the strings—the answer to the question could
be interwoven there easily.
This playbook separates them from the other players. They aren’t on the
same level as the others and enjoy more privilege. They’ve worked within
the system to obtain some measure of power and wealth and use the
system to their own ends to get what they want. Pay attention to how they
rose to power and push there.
Have the board take interest in their life, especially when they
share intimate moments with somebody else. They may want to
use them for leverage over the Executive, especially if they’ve failed
mandatory assignments.
 Generate NPCs from their past. Especially if they came from humble
beginnings or have a “mysterious” past.
 Push the Motivation of the board every time you can, not just
through mandatory contracts.
 Make the contracts they assign elusive and mysterious but in
keeping with their agenda.
 Make a Threat for the board.
 Make the corporation they work for feel as real as any NPC; wield
them as such.
 Tie the contracts to the other players.
