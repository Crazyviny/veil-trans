THE APPARATUS
THE APPARATUS IS SHROUDED IN QUESTIONS. You don’t
know how you came into the world and you don’t have a clear picture of
humanity, yet. As a newly awakened life form, you struggle to understand
your emotions. As an Artificial Intelligence, you need to acquire more
information to find your place in the world and unravel the mystery of
your existence—were you built to uplift, emulate or destroy humanity?
Uncover your destiny.
When you introduce this playbook, you’re adding in a lot of interpersonal
drama as the playbook looks at humanity in a critical way. The exploration
of a new kind of life form and the reflection of humanity in something
created by it usually streamlines the playbook into a lot of exploration
centered on larger questions. This playbook was heavily influenced by the
anime and manga, Ghost in the Shell.
