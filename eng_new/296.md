Sometimes, an MC may feel they need to make extremely hard move when
a player rolls a miss. In any situation, you should make a move as hard
as you like. Examine the NPC or situation and think of it in terms of their
Motivation. If that impulse leads them to make an extremely hard move,
so be it. If they try to Sway someone and miss, it shouldn’t always mean
that this person turns into a Threat or takes an aggressive action. Always
frame it as how much agency should this character be left with when I’m
done making my move. What is most interesting to me, the story, and the
other players.
An opportunity is something that’s been stated in the fiction already and
they’re not dealing with it or missed it. If they’re being reckless, cocky, or
daring when a soft move (or two) have shown this, then they roll a miss.
Well…that’s it my friend. On a platter right to you, no?
You can also take a miss and do something unseen by the protagonists.
Just because it’s not on screen doesn’t mean the miss didn’t happen
in a form they’re as yet unaware of. Maybe a Threat that was looming
manifests. Maybe a consequence of their success is the move to make
on a miss—where once there would have been no consequence from the
success, it is turned back on them, so to speak.
When a protagonist rolls a miss you can hold that miss. Maybe the most
interesting thing to have happen is for them to succeed despite the miss
and the suspense that comes from holding it serves a useful purpose
in the fiction. Off-screen badness could be happening right now with
that miss and they’d never know; on the other hand, maybe something
more direct will happen soon, when you have a moment to think or the
opportunity finally presents itself. Do as you like with your moves, so long
as it is in keeping with your Agenda and Principles.
