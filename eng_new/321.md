On the next page there are ideas and thoughts about each playbook.
Use that information to inform your fiction and why the player might be
attracted to that playbook, as well as what they might be after in the
fiction because of their choice. I’m sure the players will come up with
really neat spins on the playbooks, so you’ll have to roll with it and see
where it takes you.
Pay specific attention to the following fictional flags players give you:
 Their playbook. They picked it because they wanted to interact with
the world in the way they envisioned the playbook.
 Their cybernetics and their negative Tags. When something comes
up a miss, always bear in mind that there are these negative
Tags on most of them, waiting there for you. Similarly, weapons
have drawback tags and range Tags. Can their weapon be used
in the manner they wish right now, based on what you’ve said
about the fiction?
 Their Jam: how they make Cred. Do they want to be doing the thing
that makes them Cred? Usually. If not, Jam is also used as a good
way of tying other players together and establish the kind of social
structure they want and fit into. It also tells you what kind of fictional
positioning they want to begin with for their character.
 The relationships they choose to make with the Link move will show
you what kinds of things they want to be exploring in the fiction.
 Their Look and Gear, they’re telling you what they think looks cool
and what type of weapons they like. Does everyone dress like that?
Why or why not? Ask them to detail their weapons, what do they
look like? What are they called? What damage types exist?
