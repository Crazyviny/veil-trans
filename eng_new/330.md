THE HONORBOUND
The Honorbound is a character that defines Obligation within the game.
It can become very different with this playbook in play because a lot of
the fiction’s interpersonal play will come from Obligation. If Obligation is
involved consider asking them about it. Not that other people’s opinions on
Obligation don’t matter, but the main focus will be from them specifically
as that is their thing.
They will probably be the only one who feels naturally responsible and
have a strong sense of justice, either radical or passive. Pay attention to
how they themselves approach the resolution of their missions. Do they
owe Obligation, too? How do they themselves perceive honor and debt
with their actions? What are they interested in seeing for punishments and
what is important enough to warrant Obligation in the first place. These
answers will define facets of the culture that are very important.
 Make a Threat for the organization/institute they work for if they
a Motivation they intend to pursue (beyond simply carrying out
Obligation).
 Push Obligation into the fiction more predominately than you may
have otherwise.
 Make an NPC that needs guidance specifically about Obligation and
used to showcase the burden the Honorbound has in their task.
 Make their Obligation missions feel like it’s the most important
thing to them. They have to enforce things that maybe they
don’t want to, but feel they must. They’ve selected the penalties
themselves so dig into why they chose what they did. What impact
do the carry culturally?
 Riff off of their mission selections and Obligation choices to get the
right tone they’re looking for.
