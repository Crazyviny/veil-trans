At the start of the session roll and add your object’s Hunger. On a 10+,
the MC Holds 3. On a 7–9, the MC Holds 1. Throughout the session, the
MC can spend their Hold 1 for 1 to:
 Name something; your object must have it. When you give in to your
object, mark experience and -1 Hunger.
 Have it object to a course of action or propose one of its own;
Should you obey, mark experience and -1 Hunger.
If the MC has any Hold left at the end of the session, give your object
+1 Hunger, to a maximum Hunger of +4. If your object has a Hunger of
+4, take -1 Ongoing to all dice results until you find a way to satiate your
object’s Hunger. You may proposition the MC for a way to fix the issue,
or ask the MC to propose something. If the MC is neglecting to use Hold
in a session, find appropriate moments in the fiction that suggest a good
time to spend Hold and remind the MC that they can do so.
The Hunger of an object is simply what it wants. It could be scared or it
could be a sentient sword that craves the blood of its enemies. Whatever
it is, it has an agenda and its Hunger is the way in which it expresses to
you what it needs or wants.
When the MC names something it could mean a lot of things. Maybe it
just wants something nearby, it wants the blood of an enemy or it wants
to escape, and so on. Whatever it wants, if you satisfy the object’s Hunger,
the Hunger decreases by 1 and you also get XP. Alternatively, the object
may disagree or disapprove of a certain course of action, or it may propose
a course of action that it wants you to take. If you do, you get to mark XP
and decrease its Hunger by 1.

Hold to spend whenever they choose during the session.
