THE HONED
You’re the same decaying matter as everybody else. The difference
between you and them? You’ve taken it and honed it into the precision
instrument you now wield to achieve your will. You are not a part of the
system; you are free and you strive—for what, is up to you now.
The playbook in itself subverts some tropes of cyberpunk, in that they
have shirked the technological completely in preference of their human
potential. The Honed also will impact the setting in that their humanity is
gained by physical resolutions in the fiction. They’re saving people, doing
impossible feats, and undertaking difficult tasks. Although there are a lot
of deeper issues that could be brought to bear, especially with man vs
machine coming into play, most of the time, its introduction will be for the
type of player that wants to resolve conflicts physically.
A good touchstone for this playbook would be Data Runner, by Sam A.
Patel. In which a young man has no augmentations at all and uses the
philosophy at the heart of parkour to live his life, make a living, and rebel
against the systemic problems in his area. Also, the Mirror’s Edge games
touch on some aspects of this playbook.
Within the confines of the playbook, in that, they are mostly about
accomplishing a task that is going to probably be physical in nature.
Is a large variety of ways to frame a protagonist. Fundamentally, they
value freedom and the freedom of others. They have tied their worth to
accomplishing tasks and honed their body for this purpose. Why they do
so and what this means and represents for themselves and others in the
world of your fiction is something you can flesh out through play.

As the Honed, it’s important you know that you are not a part of The
Veil. You are not in mixed reality like everyone else is. You see the world
for what it truly is instead of what’s been broadcasted to your mind like
everyone else. You’re apart from the system. Apart from everyone, in that
way. No cybernetics and no Neurochip. But that does not make you lesser
than them, you are able to do feats as though you are enhanced. But be
aware that when you attempt to Lift The Veil to get information, you do
not have the same fictional positioning that always being connected to
something like the Internet affords. You’ll have to use tech to do that.
There are both benefits and drawbacks to not being connected that should
be weighed, talked about, and incorporated into your fiction.
