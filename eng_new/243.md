THE SEEKER
The power of the Seeker comes from their Faith. Faith can be many
things to them, but through it they gain Enlightenment. They use their
Enlightenment in order to have the meaningful questions in their lives
answered, they are ceaselessly dedicated in their lives to the cycle
of their Faith.
While The Veil already has some setting assumptions geared towards the
drive of getting more information, this playbook injects the possibility of
people looking for knowledge via introspection. Even in the future people
need knowledge to gain Insight. This playbook directly infuses that concept
into the setting naturally because the Seeker is primarily searching for
these answers internally. When they do, we find out what those answers
look like in a futuristic setting, as well as how someone of faith may choose
to look. Does faith appear radically different? Or is it something we identify
with now, which has been preserved over generations? The Seeker is an
opportunity to put a lens on the give and take between someone’s belief
systems and how often these real life philosophical questions come up
and are answered organically in someone’s life. Through play, they are
often answered simply because you are looking out for them in the fiction.
