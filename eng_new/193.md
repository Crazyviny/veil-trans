THE EXECUTIVE
Though a part of a larger established entity, the Executive answers directly
to their peers and, ultimately, their own agenda. But within that framework
you work within the system to realize your own goals. Balancing the line
of keeping others happy while using the resources at hand, you do what
you do best: game the system.
Most predominantly, the Executive injects a cyberpunk trope of the
megacorporation or some evolution thereof, drawing on what we classify
of as “big business” today. The trope is slightly subverted in that you
answer to this megacorporation’s board, instead of running the whole
corp yourself.
With the introduction of this playbook in the setting, you’ll also have a
stronger emphasis on Cred, naturally shifting focus onto the reliance of
it and, possibly, consumerism itself. Think the TV show Incorporated, but
with a bit more flexibility and the ability to customize and flesh out what
that corporation may be like; as well as what those people running it
actually want, beyond goals purely synonymous with capitalism. It is all
about Capital in this playbook regardless of your choices, you need more
of it and work to obtain it. The more you have the easier you moves. The
power you can exercise and grow, is all rooted in Cred and Capital.
