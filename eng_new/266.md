come out where you intend. On a 7–9, you are met with a challenge, a
complication, or a danger on the other side.

not be worth it at all, danger or a challenge awaits you there, after all. Both

the fictional positioning it affords you is also much more than a typical
playbook move. What this looks like in the fiction is up to you, so long as
you meet the requirement of your Homeland being the medium in which
this takes place.
