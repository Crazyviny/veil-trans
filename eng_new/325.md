THE CATABOLIST
When someone selects the Catabolist, they probably like the idea of free
cybernetics. Now, how they choose to go about that will be interesting.
Are they a creep who knocks people out and takes their cybernetics in
the back room of an antique shop? Or are they trying to spread the word
that cybernetics are needed to survive and wants to persuade people. Are
they scrounging about for them?
It could be a lot of things, but the main thread is power. They want it for
free. So show them it has a cost. They probably have lots of negative Tags
after getting cybernetics integrated into themselves a few times. They have
the Omni-tool that they invented to help them get it, so they’re predisposed
to liking things over people right off the bat as well. Their ideology will tell
you a lot about who they are.
 Showcase either the value of cybernetics or the downside of them,
depending on their ideology.
 Poke and Prod at their Ideology as though it is a Belief. Test it.
 Integrate the ideology into society and culture.
 Make an NPC in contrast to their ideology that’s either likable and
likes them or is the opposite; decide based on what makes sense
for the NPCs already made and their Motivations.
 If they use Things Speak make sure to incorporate answers into the
fiction as though they were answers from a named NPC.
