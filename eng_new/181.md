F F THE DYING SPECIAL
When the Dying shares a moment of intimacy with someone, be it
physical or emotional, they generate 1 Hold. Whenever your disease
would Trigger, you may instead spend this Hold to keep the Symptoms
at bay by recalling the moment of intimacy.
In every case when intimacy might be happening, the player works with the
MC to determine what an intimate moment is to them, and to one another.
A character opening up about something deeply troubling or even a burden
they’ve yet to disclose, could be a big deal for them, but not for the other
person. If a player thinks their special should trigger the table should pay
attention to why they feel it should, then clarify the fiction if needed. If
you do feel like a particularly intimate moment has taken place, bring it
up and ask the MC and other players if they think so, too. If they do, the
special occurs and the recollection of that moment in the future can help
the Dying to suppress a trigger from happening in the future. The Dying
is more likely to encounter situations like this based on the timeline and
content within the playbook. Never be afraid to advocate for this move
going off, as it is very subjective.
