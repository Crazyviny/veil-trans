THE HONORBOUND
The primary drive for the Honorbound is their dedication to enforcing
others to hold up their Obligation within society. In The Veil, this form
of honor is ingrained into the culture in which you play and you are the
keeper of it. This is a fictional culture you all are portraying, part of it is
this mechanic and system for Obligation. It is not something to be set
aside. It is something depicted within the lifestyle in this future. It’s easy
to forget about as comradeship between teammates is common. But this
is cyberpunk, so a lot of times people will need their proverbial strings
pulled in order to do what you want. Why would ordinary folks help you
resist the system and risk getting into trouble themselves?
Obligation is the answer. Some people may help you in your goals,
others may not. By establishing how this culture feels about specific
actions and what entitles people to reciprocate; we get a clearer picture
of the “average” person in this fictional cyberpunk world, and how you
differ from them.
The Honorbound injects a much more focused lens on Obligation. What
this future looks like when we interrogate it from this perspective is up to
you. The Honorbound gets to define the parameters of Obligation pretty
much in its entirety. You’ll clarify a lot of what might otherwise remain
nebulous and also bring both interpersonal play—because there’s that
narrowed lens looking at how all people treat their Obligations— and
what is even considered an Obligation; as well as what enforcement
and authority in general will look like in the setting. The relationship
between this enforcement and perhaps another form of authority is also
something to be considered when introducing this playbook into the mix.
The playbook will help you clarify all of this, don’t worry, just work through
it choosing your options.
