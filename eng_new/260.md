F F NOURISH
When you attempt to nourish your Homeland, roll. On a 10+, choose 2.
On a 7–9, choose 1.
 You halt the erosion where it stands, for a time.
 You begin to restore what’s already gone.
 You gain 2 Essence.
Nourishing your Homeland can mean a lot of things based on your
previous selections. There’s a ton of options but whatever it is you chose
when filling out the selection is what you’ve defined as the trigger of the
move. You can halt the erosion for a limited time, begin to restore what’s
already gone, or you can gain 2 Essence. Not choosing the Erosion halts
and not choosing to restore it means that, at the MC’s discretion—your
Homeland will continue to suffer. Note that just the act of trying to cultivate
and Nourish it is enough to get Essence, but it does not actually help it,
rather only you stand to gain if you do not choose to give up what you
stand to gain; assuming you roll well.
