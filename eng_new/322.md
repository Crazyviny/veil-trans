PLAYBOOK FOCUSES
THE APPARATUS
The Apparatus is a character that’s looking for their place in the world.
They are new and is trying to understand where it belongs, if anywhere.
They’re probably naive in comparison to most humans, lacking information.
They are constantly asking questions every session about their past and
who they are. There are a lot of angles they could take the playbook on.
Give them an outlet in which to discuss those thoughts that they have or
a thread to pull on for their self-exploration. The more questions they ask,
the more Humanity they generate and spends.
The most identifiable thing you’ll see about what direction they’re taking
the playbook is what they choose to look like. If they choose to look
entirely different than a human, chances are they aren’t looking to fit in.
In contrast, if they choose to look completely human and can reproduce,
vice versa, they want to fit in, and probably begin to form a relationship
with people in order to look into what humanity has to offer.
It can be a pretty powerful playbook. They are fully cybernetic and could
choose be really dangerous. Whether or not they want to be dangerous is
the question. Ultimately, most people will probably use it as an exploratory
playbook, looking into the abyss and asking questions about themselves,
humanity, and struggling to become more than they are now.
