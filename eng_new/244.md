 The area titled “The Faith.”
The Faith section is where you’ll define what your Faith really is; pragmatic,
violent, etc. Work down the sheet selecting the options. Keep in mind
that these options will determine a major portion of your character. If you
do not act within the confines of the Faith you’re selecting now, it can
hinder you in the future and become unfun. In a way you are choosing
how you want your protagonist to act by what they adhere to. When you
roll your Communion move at the start of each session, the result dictates
if you get surplus, want, or both. Each decision changes what you may
get each session.
Enlightenment is the resource and the modifier you use in the Communion
move, so the more Enlightenment you have the more likely you will get
surplus and not want. Surplus is something positive for you whereas
want is something that is demanded by your Faith that is framed a little
bit more negatively. No matter what choice you select make sure it’s
something interesting to you for both choices as either could be in your
fiction, depending on your roll. If the pair are interesting you’ll be happy
no matter the outcome since you’ll be exploring what you want in the
fiction regardless.
By default your Faith begins with:
1 ENLIGHTENMENT: This is used as a unique currency for this

playbook. You gain Enlightenment, which you spend at the beginning of
the session to Commune with your Faith, and that in turn grants you your
surplus and want for the game

SURPLUS:1 ANSWER: You can “cash in” 1 answer to ask a question.

When you ask a question (from the section marked questions) you’re
telling the MC what you want to see in this session of play. What you’re
hoping to drive your character towards and get out of play. You’re cashing
in your answer at the start of the play, but it remains in effect until you
spend another answer to change it to something else. You’re seeking your
answer throughout the entirety of the session, not right away as soon as
you spend it.
WANT: PENANCE: When and if your want comes up in the game, the

MC will work to incorporate this into the fiction. If you need to do penance,
the MC and you will have a discussion about what that will look like and
if it should be on screen or not. Either way, it should be something that
affects your protagonist and would not simply hand wave. There should
be at least one scene about it. It doesn’t need to be the main focus of the
session but it should definitely be a thing that is now introduced and has
a real impact on the fiction and characterizes you.
