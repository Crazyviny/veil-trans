Underneath the Faith section, there is a box where you can describe your
Faith based on your selections. This way you can describe it, right down
your want, surplus, Enlightenment, and the Cred you get from your Faith,
if applicable.
JAK E: I’m going to be a Seeker named Hugo. He’s like a monk,

who upholds the weak. I think the options that make
the most sense are going to be that he’s peaceful,
pragmatic, and the Faith is all about mortality. I’m
going to choose:
When you meditate, you do so fervently.
+1 Enlightenment
Your meditation is deep and spiritual, surplus:
+1 answer, replace want: penance with want:
disconnected
Your meditations require strict fasting. Replace want:
penance with want: hunger
And Hugo’s Faith must be spread zealously, want
+preach.
In the boxes on the character sheet, under each category, Jake wrote:
Description: My Faith is one of peaceful pragmatism
that centers on mortality.
Surplus: 1 answer and 1 Cred
Want: disconnected, hunger, and preach.
Cred: nothing yet.
He doesn’t have any Cred to begin with. When he does the beginning of
session roll, and if does well, he will generate some by getting his surplus
and put it here.
Enlightenment: 2 Enlightenment
Jake does well on his roll, he gets 1 Cred and can seek an answer. If he
had done poorly on the roll and generated want, he would have had to
choose between disconnected, hunger, and preach. Then, the MC would
show in the fiction what that would look like. Maybe something causes him
to lose connection with his Faith, causing a disconnect. Maybe his Faith
demands that he fasts during a period of the session, how that would
affect his character in game mechanically would be up to the MC and
him to work out. Otherwise it could be framed as characterizing scenes
for this purpose.
