As you play your first game, everyone at the table (but the MC in particular)
will get to know what you and your friends are looking to get out of the
game. What interests you, and where to take the fiction after that first
session. Most of the time will be spent world building and introducing the
protagonists to one another but make sure you set aside enough time so
that there can be some gameplay as well. At this point, you’re not worrying
about telling a cohesive story aimed at being part of a larger campaign.
Your goal this session is simply to get to know the game world and the
people that live there, including the protagonists.
If you’re the MC, you can skip to the section named Master of Ceremonies
(page 278). If you’re a player, the first thing you’ll need to do is pick a
playbook. A playbook is a character sheet with a set of moves specific to
that character type and some specific information that will help define
your protagonist you choose and fill out.
