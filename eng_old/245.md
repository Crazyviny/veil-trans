Answers tell the MC what you want to be looking for within the fiction of
the game, which will then drive the fiction in that direction. If he does
poorly on the roll and generates want, he chooses between disconnected,
hunger, and preach. Then, the MC would show in the fiction what that
would look like. Maybe something causes him to lose connection with his
