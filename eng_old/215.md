responsible, while they remain present and hostile, you get +1 hold on
a hit whenever you try to neutralize them.

someone’s hacking you up and you’re attempting to get more hold on
someone random, which has nothing to do with what’s happened, the MC
isn’t going to be able to help you out with supporting your move.
