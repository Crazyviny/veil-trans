The place to fill in cybernetics is there for the player to detail any cybernetic
enhancements they want their protagonist to start with, and how they got
them. Depending on what the player chooses, they are able to detail that
piece of cybernetics by assigning it a number of tags equal to the number
in the brackets beside it. Finally, any piece of cybernetics must also be
assigned a negative tag.
 A section that details a number of special moves a protagonist both
starts out with, and may acquire through improvement.
The section that details the special moves a protagonist has can be found
on the right-hand side. Beside each move there is a circle; if a circle is
already marked in by default, it means that a protagonist begins play with
that move, which is aptly referred to as a starting move. There are moves
listed below it and those that have empty circles are acquired later on in
play when a protagonist gains enough experience to grow and advance.
