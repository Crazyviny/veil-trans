F F JUNKWARE
When you use your Omni-tool to scan for viable scrounged materials and
incorporate cybernetics into yourself, roll+Omni-tool. On a 10+, choose
2. On a 7–9, choose 1:
 You create the cybernetic implant with one tag.
 It does not create a negative tag.
 It does not harm you.
How you incorporate these scrounged materials into your person, remember
that within the fiction, to do it, you do it. You have to describe what you do for
a move to trigger, always. How you use the Omni-tool and your smarts to get
this stuff to work, make sure you make it evocative so the whole table gets a
clear idea of what you have in your head when you do this. As with some other
moves, you’re choosing what does not happen—whatever you do not choose
could happen to you, depending on what call the MC makes.

choose one, right—uh-oh! I’m going to choose to create
a cybernetic implant, at least then I got it. I think the
emotion around the scene is going to be Powerful.
It sucks that there’s going to be some feedback or
something, but this is his wheelhouse after all—so I
think he’s feeling confident.

the systems come online for it and what not—but its old

sense that it’s unreliable. But it’s up to you.

stuff to integrate, the process damages my surrounding

up with this door. Can I walk up to it and scan it and see
what’s up with it?
