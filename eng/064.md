F F HELP OR HINDER
When you act to help or impede another player character, say how you
do so and roll. On a hit, they take +1 (assist) or -2 (impede) to their roll.
On a 7–9, you also expose yourself to danger, retribution or a cost.
This move is used solely when another protagonist is doing something and
you want to modify the outcome of what they’re doing. If your teammate is
about to shoot an innocent or do something you think is wrong, you could
actively try to have that not happen. If they’re trying to take out some giant
mechanized tank that’s about to do some serious harm, you’d probably
roll to help them out.
