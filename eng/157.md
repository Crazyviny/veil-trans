F F ATTACHED SPECIAL
When the Attached shares a moment of intimacy with someone, be it
physical or emotional, roll+ your object’s Hunger. On a 10+, they take -1
Forward and you take -1 Ongoing until the object believes it was no big
deal. On a 7–9, It Speaks triggers. On a miss, give your object -1 Hunger.
This is truly the move that shows the nature of your relationship, or at
least, the one it believes it has with you even if you don’t. The intention is
to show that though it is an object, the intimacy with someone else also
impacts your relationship with it. Your own intimate moments are handled
in the fiction entirely; mechanically what we are figuring out is the reaction
of your object.
MC: So Deacon and you are up there shooting the shit,

and you tell him all about building Gizmo. He gets
this familiar look in his eye, like he’s a little Sad. He
looks up and takes another last swig of that god-awful
moonshine you guys made, polishing it off. He looks at
you and says, “The only thing we’ve got left after Ray
being killed is each other.” He looks you straight in the
eyes. What do you do?
BOB: I mean, Ray just died, I’m emotional and vulnerable

and can see Deacon is, too. Gizmo’s been my life,
though; since Ray’s death. No one really understands
Gizmo’s and mine relationship, heck I’m not sure I do.
I’ve been keeping Deacon at arm’s length but I think
at this point that facade just crumbles. I don’t want to
have a scene with it or anything but I’ll make a move.
Assuming he still feels the same way, we can cut to my
place or his place.
MC: Sure, dude. No problem. Before we do that though,

let’s activate your special. Deacon doesn’t have one
because he’s an NPC.
BOB: Oh right. OK, well, I’m feeling appreciated I think, so I’ll

mark a Spike in Powerful here. I got an 11 when seeing
how Gizmo feels about all this, though—greeeat.
MC: So, you take -1 Ongoing until Gizmo believes it’s no big

deal. Time for an awkward conversation…
