THE ARCHITECT
The Architect lives and breathes The Veil. You have a powerful Cyberbrain
that you can use to change aspects of digital environments at will.
Most predominantly, the Architect injects finer lens on specific aspects of
The Veil and directly injects the completely digital world, or digiscape. The
manifestation of your own subconscious into this digital space because
of your manipulations, and the ramifications thereof, is the main source
of discovery and tension in this playbook. A mix of Inception and The
Matrix themes are the main source of inspiration. You will get a lot of
creative license with your moves, specifically in the digital world—and
because of this, the setting will have an emphasis on this space than it
otherwise might have.

The Architect is at home and wields more power than any other protagonist
within a digital space. When you enter said space, you frame the scene
instead of the MC. What this means is that when you enter, you tell
everyone else what their own perception is of that space, and in so
doing, you’ll be able to define The Veil more than anyone else. Your own
perception of The Veil becomes their reality, at least, so far as within the
reasonable constraints of the fiction already. All other aspects of play
continue as normal after the scene is introduced by you. Of course, this
is also optional, you do not have to do this every time for every scene in
which you enter the space, but you can, should you choose to.
While in that space, you also bring parts of yourself forward. Manifestations
of your subconscious follow, when choosing the options of what is brought
forward into this place with you, you’ll see multiple options on the sheet
tell you to “name an NPC”; when an NPC is named, it means they become
an important character in the fiction, and so need a name. Keep in mind
that this means that at some point, these characters you’re introducing
will be making an appearance in your game and be portrayed by the MC.
