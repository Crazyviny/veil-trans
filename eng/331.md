THE ONOMASTIC
The Onomastic is always hunted, so make them feel that way.
They have a hugely rich past setup for you to play on. What was the order
like? How old were they when they became the last Onomastic? What
is that like? Again, no one could feel like them, so make sure you’re
exploring that.
When they hit a fifth improvement and reveal that Cybertome, make sure
that’s going somewhere. Always be probing so when it does open and you
have that conversation! Do not take away their Sanctuary, it is literally
a Sanctuary. It would have to be some extreme circumstances for it to
be taken away.
Do they introduce a mystical element? Is what they can do technological
in nature, magical, whatever. If they want to define it, let them. Then
incorporate those answers not only into the Iconoclasts, but the world at
large in order to interrogate it, if it is or becomes interesting.
 Create a Threat for the Iconoclasts.
 Create a Threat for the Cybertome, when and if applicable.
 Ask them about the order every chance you can. What was lost
beyond the individuals they knew?
 Create an NPC to reach out to them regarding their order, their grief,
thoughts on the order, etc. If the PCs don’t engage with it with them.
 Ask them to describe new parts of their Sanctuary every time they
go into it. What has changed, what haven’t we seen yet?
 Ask them how they survived this long and how they were being
hunted. Incorporate it all into the fiction.
