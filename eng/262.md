F F BEING A GREEN MOTHER
When you channel your Essence into the natural world through touch—be
it plants, animals, or anything that is a product of the earth—roll+ Essence
spent. On a 10+, choose 2. On a 7–9, choose 1.
 Your senses extend through it as though you were a part of it. You
feel what they feel, hear what they hear, etc.
 You may treat it as though it were a named NPC for the
purposes of moves.
 Choose one of the States your Homeland elicits and Spike out one
of them within the target.
Depending on where your Homeland actually is located, it may be an
extension of the main area of your setting (such as adjacent to or not
completely encapsulated by a city sprawl, etc). Whatever you touch—so
long it is of the natural world—can be the target of this move. By doing so,
you can extend your senses, that is to say reasonably hear, touch, taste,
and see what your target would be able to. You may also treat it as an NPC,
granting you the ability to use moves on something you ordinarily wouldn’t
have been able to (Probing, Analyzing, etc.). Lastly, you can Spike out the
emotion from one of your selections when you chose what emotion your
Homeland elicits within you. With all selections make sure you translate
your selections into the fiction in a way that makes sense and is evocative
for the other players at the table, as usual.
