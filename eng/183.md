THE EMPATH
The Empath is in tune with the people around them and their emotions.
They interact with emotions as tangible, charged energy fields that can
be manipulated and appropriated for their own purposes.
Whether this ability is uncanny or futuristic, or explained at all is up to
you, but as with all playbooks it beings assumptions about the setting.
Are these magical abilities or are their explanation needed at all? Are they
using technology or biology if it needs to be explained, both? Regardless
of the explanation, the Empath also will most likely focus on interpersonal
play due to its nature, as well as dealing with emotional burdens and their
tole on an individual—should you wish to touch on the subject matter.
