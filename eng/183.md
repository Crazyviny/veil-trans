THE EMPATH
The Empath is in tune with the people around them and their emotions.
They interact with emotions as tangible, charged energy fields that can
be manipulated and appropriated for their own purposes.
Whether this ability is uncanny or futuristic, or explained at all, is up to
you, but as with all playbooks it brings assumptions about the setting.
Where do these abilities come from? Are their explanation needed or
wanted in your fiction? If yes, are they using technology, biology, or they
fantastical? Regardless of the explanation, the Empath also will most
likely focus on interpersonal play due to its nature. With lots of play time
in fiction dealing with the emotional burdens people carry with them—both
yours and others—and their toll on an individual.
The Empath engages with the idea of consent and what it might look like
in your fiction directly. Especially when dealing with a playbook that can
directly affect other protagonists and players with their moves. You can get
your resource, Flow, in many ways, some of which directly breach consent.
Because of this, there is some additional language on the playbook about
it. Be open at the table about how you intend to play your character and
make sure that will be fun for you, the table, and your fiction.
