MC: Alright, you’re in the ward. As the smoking man said

there would be, there are rows and rows of people
sleeping. Some young; some old. It’s an all-white, sterile
place, and the only sound is the machines keeping
these people alive. He asked you to save these people
as he believes that they have a virus which doesn’t
allow them to dream. He’s got you tethered to this
locale with that device he put on you before he let you
out of the prison. In exchange for curing these people,
he will release you. Here they are. What do you do?
KIER: Right, I’ll drift over to one of them and propagate

some dreams. I think how I manufacture dreams is
probably by telling a story. Not just any story, either.
It just comes from my lips. I don’t know the words or
remember them—it’s just something my people do. We
can discern individuals and then speak their story to
them. It almost always certainly makes them dream. I
think I’m Peaceful, there’s a reassurance in tradition
that calms people and I think what I’m doing resembles
that. With Peaceful, I got an 8. So it works at least?
That’s good. I like to think that maybe as my lips move
with the story of this person, eventually their lips start
moving,too—mouthing the same words as my own lips
start to move. And I think as they mouth these words
an imperceptible dust comes out of their mouth and it
flows into my helmet, like I’m drinking it or something –
which gives me 2 Essence. I now have 4 Essence since
I began with 2.
MC: I like that image a lot, very cool. So that totally happens

and you get your Essence, everything seems to be going
really well. But, I think this time you hear what they
say in the last sentence of this specific ritual you’re
exercising here. You think you hear them whisper “close
the door,“ and then they smile faintly and drift off into
a slumber. It seems to have worked, so what do you do
now? One down, a LOT to go.
