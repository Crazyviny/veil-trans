BE AWARE
When you’re introducing new content into the fiction, always think about
what would be predictable. What has society and media concocted already
for you to interact with and think about at every turn and think about
what your spin is. What it is like in the future is up to you and the other
players. It’s still probably hard and horrible, life always is for protagonists
in stories—but maybe instead of the procedural media we now watch,
switch it up anytime you can think of something more interesting. See
if the players dig it and roll with it if so. When they’re seeing the events
unfold, push them to discuss with each other what they think is going on
and make it specifically not that, unless doing so is the more interesting
thing to do. Try not to go too much into clichés and what has already been
done, this is the future and it’s up to everyone at the table to defy these
constraints on the genre. Be Punks.

PICK YOUR MOMENTS
When you MC, sometimes you may be tempted to believe that the
moments that are mired with tragedy are the most important, you’re
wrong. It should be dark and gritty in order to then have the human
aspects of the story be the players themselves. It’s most interesting to
find out as much as you can about a protagonist. To watch them grow in
their Beliefs and to see what they confide in, who they confide in, and what
questions they bring up in play about themselves and the world. Conflict
that have cool fight scenes are great but the fights only matter if we have
context. Discover the why of it. The system beats them down so that they
find the humanity, the light, the good in one another. If they can’t see that
and it’s always bleak—it’s emotionally draining and probably not much
fun in the long run. Coaxing hope and solace and joy from the players in
an otherwise harsh world though, will be a story you’ll always remember,
cool fights or not.
