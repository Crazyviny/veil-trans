When you gain Insight, you ask your Faith what the best course of action
is in relation to the question you are seeking and the MC will tell you. Take
+1 Forward towards rolls in the pursuit of that information. This is the
only way to achieve Insight in The Veil; though protagonists may obviously
seek insight from other characters as normal, it does not provide them
with +1 Forward when acting on the advice.
Insight is something unique to the Seeker. You can gain an advantage
to your next action when you consult with it. This gives you +1 Forward.
This Insight is different from asking other people, it is reserved for the
interaction with your faith and only if you take +insight as a surplus option.
