F F RECONFIGURE
When you remake parts of the digital environment as you see fit,
describe what you take from the digital world and what it becomes,
then roll+Cyberbrain. On a 10+, you do it and all 3. On a 7–9, you do it
but you may only choose 1.
 You do not suffer feedback.
 The change is not noticed.
 Your subconscious stays repressed.
When an Architect remakes the digital environment, they must take
something from it and change it into something else. Think of it like the
law of conservation of mass, even in the digital space—you cannot simply
destroy or produce something from nothing. What they use to create
something new does not have to be similar in any way. You might want to
embellish details about what this process looks like too, like what is done
with any excess mass used when you change stuff, for instance; but it’s
up to you, as assumptions can be made. Maybe you decide to change a
car into a bicycle and describe the rest of the car’s mass dissipating as
the bicycle is shaped from it. Or, maybe you draw mass out of the car and
it becomes some kind of a spinning ball, which you then channel outward
to become this bicycle. Whatever you decide to do, make sure to make
it evocative for everyone else at the table. Enjoy the creative license the
moves afford and the agency you have in the fiction to do cool things!
Instead of telling the MC you change the color of the walls so that you
blend in better, say that you slam your hand against the wall and a ripple
shoots outward, changing the color of the wall as it moves along!
Each choice on this particular move ensures that something “bad”
does not happen to your character. That is, if you can only choose one
of the these things to not happen to you, as per the 7–9 result, then
the other two things do happen. Of course, it’s up to the MC if the two
things happen right away, or later, or if only one of the things happen,
they have control—but from a player standpoint it is safe to assume what
you do not choose will come into the fiction. It’s their job to have some
interesting consequences happen in the fiction, so they’ll choose what
works best at the time.
Important: even though you’re adding the modifier from your Cyberbrain,
you still mark an emotion Spike in whichever State your protagonist is
experiencing at that moment.
