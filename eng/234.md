 The area titled “Components”
Onomastics have a kind of power over others completely unlike any other
playbook. It could be considered supernatural or it could be a form of
technology that your order engineered. The supernatural and what could
be considered “magic” can also be labeled as such simply because we do
not understand it yet. Maybe you’re so far beyond normal people it seems
like magic, but is actually not. However you decide to explain it in the
fiction, the more you know of someone—specifically major life moments,
the more power you have over them. You have the power to manipulate
the people you have Components on in various ways. When you use your
power, you use these Components in order to exert your control. You can
only hold three at a time and can manage them however you like, but your
maximum shouldn’t exceed that. Spend and accrue them wisely!
