F F GUIDANCE
When you share your vision of the future and how you plan to achieve
it, roll+Enlightenment. On a 10+, Hold 3 over them. On a 7–9, Hold 2
over them. Whenever you like, you can spend your Hold, 1 for 1, to have
them mark experience when they do something to further your vision.
To do it, you do it means you must say your plan and how you will achieve
it, specifically in relation to your vision for the future. Not just any plan
and not anything so vague as to just be an idea, with no real plan being
expressed. You are trying to get the other character on board with your
vision, your philosophy, your ideals and ideology, etc. To do that generally
requires some candor and some heart; or a deft manipulation, on
the other hand.
JAKE: Well, at least she’s safe. I grip your hand and move it

off my shoulder, then shake it. I get a sad look in my
eyes and launch into a story, and begin to tell you of a
time when I will no longer see this senseless violence
and have no need for my sword, at all. I, too need to
remember to not resort to violence as often as I do.
MC: Hmm, sounds like some Guidance, sorta, kinda? If that’s

what you’re trying to do, you’ll need to give me more
than that. You’re trying to show your dreams and hopes
here with these words when you’re telling him about the
future you want.
JAKE: Right, right. Makes sense. Hmm. Well, I think I see a

future that looks a lot different than this one, that’s
for sure. I see a place where poor sods like that don’t
have to steal just to survive. Where there’s more of the
green out there than small, little patches of it scattered
around. I’m going to find the answers I need and share
them with the world, one-by-one, if I have to. Violence
against one another tore both of our families apart,
after all. This can’t be how we were meant to live, it
can’t be. Right? I resolve to try to never do violence for
such a stupid reason again. It is hard effecting change.
I’ll also tell you of what I am seeking today as I was
meditating only a few minutes ago.
MC: That’s more like it, so let’s see that Guidance roll.
