CREATING AND
DEPICTING NPCS
Whenever you think there ought to be a character right now to interact
with (for whatever reason) there is. If there feels like a space in the
protagonists’ lives that needs someone or something there, create
it. Name them always, to make them real, both for you, as well as the
protagonists. Then give them a Motivation. When you do that, just make
them follow that Motivation, regardless. They either get the thing, or they
don’t. In broad strokes that is all you need to know about them. If they
become more than this to serve a function of the fiction, so be it. But their
inception is simply because the story needs them to serve a function.
They need to be portrayed as people and not caricatures of people. But
what they want and need is distilled into a Motivation they follow to reduce
cognitive load for you. It also points them at something, whether that is
a protagonist or another NPC, the result is some kind of an interaction;
possibly drama.
