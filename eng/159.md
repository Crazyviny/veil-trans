THE CATABOLIST
The Catabolist is obsessed with integrating cybernetic systems into their
own body but rejects all conventional cybernetic processes. Only through
their own means are they able to take tech, modify it and make use of
it. They revere technology, whether to rise mankind up or rip them down
to make way for the new age. They are also highly intelligent and have
created an Omni-tool that helps them to salvage and modify tech to further
their own ends.
Most likely the introduction of the playbook inject some moral questions.
How far will you go to get the technology you need so badly for your
ideology? A new kind of tech, alternative to the standard cybernetics
or standard technologies could become a big deal in the setting, too.
This playbook as well as the Attached brings with it the implication that
you understand objects more than people in some ways, especially with
specific moves available to you.
