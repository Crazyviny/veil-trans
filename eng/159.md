THE CATABOLIST
The Catabolist, obsessed with integrating cybernetic systems into your own
body which, cruelly rejects all conventional means of getting cybernetics.
Only through your own means are you able to take tech, modify it, and
make use of it. You revere technology, whether to rise mankind up or rip it
down in order to make way for the new age; crafting an omni-tool to further
their own ends. You craft your own cybernetics and integrate them into
your body; carving your own destiny into your very flesh.
Most likely the introduction of this particular playbook will inject some
moral questions. How far will you go to get the technology you need so
badly, based on your ideology? A new kind of tech, alternative to the
standard cybernetics or standard technologies could become a big
deal in the setting, too. As you create your own cybernetics, apart from
whatever else other people use, which you detail yourself. This playbook
brings with it the implication that you understand technology more than
people, at least in some ways; specific moves available to you have strong
implications for both you and the setting.
The Catabolist could be similar to Tetsuo: The Iron Man; in which a man
who fetishizes metal becomes the thing he desires, to disastrous effect.
The playbook is accommodating of a wide range of interpretations.
