What is the agenda of The Veil? You probably won’t find out for a few
sessions. But it is a give and take relationship between the players. They’re
going to want information from it constantly. Make access to The Veil have
a price, or be as equally curious about them. Then leave space to find out
why through play.
Because they perceive only what The Veil allows them to and they must
otherwise lift that veil with a move, you have a lot of creative freedom.
What do they want to see? What does The Veil obfuscate? What could
be the possible reason(s) it would do so? These answers will help you to
define what “it” is.
Is a tool of the system or something within that system with it’s own
agenda, power, and agency? Everything should be thought of in terms
of either a part of the system, or not. Resisting or Complying. Helping or
harming. How can it reflect your Final Question, or pose it?
A recurring theme of cyberpunk is that there are forces at play that can’t
possibly be understood or manipulated. Elements of this should manifest
within The Veil. It does not need to be malicious to do this. The sense that
there is this presence should be there, though. It perpetuates the fiction
because apart from everything else, there is still something unknowable
they interact with on a daily basis. It is the thing that pulls the wool over
everyone’s eyes or the conduit used by someone or something else in
order to do so.
Consider also, tech like in the movie Inception, where the protagonists
could enter people’s dreams. A space where the digital world the players
could enter, except that the person’s mind was paying attention to their
intrusion is compelling fiction. When they defy the constraints of reality in
there, the projections would pay attention and the environment would fight
back. Within the setting playbook, there is a specific section for adding
technological and cultural aspects you’d like to see in play. Adding that
to your world, depending on the fiction you all are going for, may make
sense, or it might not. But using a digital space to explore concepts like
those in Inception makes for a good springboard when you think about
what you could do with The Veil in your own game. It is another tool at the
very least. Use it.
