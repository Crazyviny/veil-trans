AGENDA
Above all else, behind every Principle and move you do, you need to make
this Agenda the root of it all. Everything stems from this:
 Make the world feel real
 Make it high tech and make it personal
 Embed the Final Question into everyone and everything
 Make the protagonists lives interesting and interconnected
 Play to find out what happens
It’s the job of the MC to do all of these things. No one wants to take part
in a boring conversation. Luckily, it’s a lot easier than it looks. The easiest
way to make the world seem real is to describe it, then ask questions
about it as often as possible. Whether you’re in a mega structure or the
future is irradiated and humans are nomads, there are people.
Though you may have your own ideas of where this may lead if they continue
their actions, remember that there is no preconceived plot that must happen;
you’re not there to hold their hand either. There is a Main Antagonist, the Final
Question (page 361), and NPC’s to drive the story. For NPC’s, sometimes their
interests may align with the protagonists, but they are governed solely by their
Motivation (page 315).
The protagonists might sometimes think they want the easy out, or the
predefined plot but they don’t. If you experiment with a plot-based system,
at best, it will not make sense when the dice dictate that there will be a
diversion in your set course. At worst, you’ll wreck the game for everybody.
People want to experience conflict and explore their characters. If nothing
bad ever happens or only bad things occur, it’s a surefire way of them
doing two things: becoming exasperated, and being unsure of what this
game is actually about. All of the mechanics facilitate a different style of
play. The system will kick their ass enough, all you need to do is make it
feel real, keep it interesting, and make sure it’s everybody’s fiction, not
just your own.
Commit to any every moment within the fiction and always follow the
Motivation of the NPC or Threat (page 315), regardless of the fallout. The
fiction will make logical sense should you do these things, so trust it; where
it’s taking you and your friends will be fun. It’s important to care about
what happens next but also distance yourself enough so that when you
need to make a move, you’re considering all of your options and choosing
the best one. When they roll the dice, be a fan of them; always. But also
bring it when they roll a miss. All of these cogs working together is the
grace of the system at play. They are the protagonists, you are the fiction.
Do your best by it.
