The Flow is the energy you get when you absorb
emotion spikes as well as the interface for your
abilities. When you charge objects, recreate past
events and interact with emotions, it’s all through
the Flow. What does it do for you (choose 1):

ABSORB: You begin with 0 Flow. When you
intentionally draw on the emotions around you, ame
someone present; if it’s a PC, choose an emotion and
absorb all their spikes. If it’s an NPC, the MC will tell
you what you can absorb from them when you roll.
On a 10+, choose 3. On a 7–9, choose 2. On a miss,
choose 1 anyway, but also take 1 harm AP.
