F F ULTIMATUM
On a 12+, your enemy has only two choices. Surrender completely or be
incapacitated by you.

F F NEUTRALIZE
On a 12+, you generate 4 Hold and take no Harm in the doing.

F F RISK
On a 12+, the possibility of Harm is out of the question. You do what
you set out to do but instead it becomes an impossible moment, an
impossible feat, a few seconds of grace, or the situation becomes
immediately advantageous.

F F ANALYZE
On a 12+, you may ask 3 questions, which now include the following:
 How secure is this place?
 What, if anything, do I notice despite an effort to conceal it?
 What here is most valuable to ____?

F F PROBE
On a 12+, Hold 3 and you may choose from any of these questions as
well:
 How could I make you feel _____?
 What, if anything, do you regret?
 What is the connection between _____ and ____?

F F LIFT THE VEIL
On a 12+, you reach beyond The Veil and in so doing, see the inner
workings of it and may ask 3 questions of the MC regarding it from the
Probe and Analyze moves, immediately.

F F DIVERT
On a 12+, all 4 happen and you take +1 Forward.

F F SWAY
On a 12+, describe how circumstances change so that you may choose
one option to apply to them, instead of you.
