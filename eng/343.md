THE MC FIRST
SESSION SHEET
The First Session sheet is a tool for you, as MC, to write down the
interesting things that came up in play that you want to explore in future
games with your players. There are spaces to write-in various important
things that could come up in play. There is also a scenario sheet that is
used to take the ideas you wrote about in the first session and put them
down on paper so that you can turn it into a more focused, well defined
second session—based on what’s already happened, which is talked about
more in-depth in the next section.
