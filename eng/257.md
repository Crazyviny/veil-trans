 The area titled “Anchor.”
Anchors are something only you can use as the Wayward. They are used
to move yourself from point A to point B. One anchor ties to one place in
the physical reality, the other to a point in your Homeland (which could be
another physical location but does not have to be), traversing the distance.
Again, whether this makes sense that it’s supernatural in your fiction or
technological in nature is up to you, dictated by the flavor and the tone
you’re going for at the table.
This section contains a move; it triggers when you tie point A to point
B and then roll to see how creating this Anchor goes. If all goes well
(10+), it’s strong and clear. On a 7–9 however, things aren’t so easy.
The options are left intentionally nebulous in order to facilitate the MC
incorporating whatever happens easily into the fiction. Since this is your
Homeland though, chances are it will be a conversation with more agency
learning towards you, as your Homeland is something you are more
knowledgeable about.
