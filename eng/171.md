THE DYING
The Dying is suffering from a non-communicable, debilitating disease that
is slowly destroying them. They may or may not be aware of their ultimate
demise, but what they are aware of is the symptoms of it. Although their
condition also grants them boons, they will succumb to it eventually. There
is no cure but there may be hope, grace, or whatever you may wish to find
at the end of your tale.
At its heart the Dying injects urgency into the game. Maybe not the first
couple sessions, but whatever you hope to accomplish is on a time frame,
regardless of your choices for how fast the disease progresses. This
infuses elements of mortality into a cyberpunk backdrop, which usually
has people living even longer due to technological advances. The uncanny
is brought forward in the form of the gifts you get from being the Dying.
How does your physiology change to accommodate these changes? Is it
something unexplained or explained with technology? Whether you choose
to introduce more of a fantastical element, or lean toward explaining it as
pertaining to the advancement of some kind of technology, it will change
the setting by having introduced either of these things with the playbook.

to be read over carefully, then filled in. These areas are:
 The area titled “The Gift.”
The gift is something that the disease of the Dying has granted you.
Though you will eventually die, you also can do incredible feats! You
may be immune to death itself...Until your disease takes you. Maybe it
facilitates your integration with cybernetics so much so that you don’t
take negative Tags for them; a powerful tool to get what you want out of
your life—while it lasts.
