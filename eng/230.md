F F HONORBOUND SPECIAL
When the Honorbound shares a moment of intimacy with someone
other than a target, be it physical or emotional, you later learn what
Obligation they owe other people and may ask them any one question
from Absolution immediately, regardless if you have taken the move
already or not.
Whenever this moment does happen, you ask the other player, or the
MC—if they have any Obligation owing and they’ll tell you. As always, make
sure you’re explaining how your character got this knowledge in the fiction
as well. On top of that, asking a question from your Absolution move is
also very powerful—especially if you’re interested in being an interpersonal
character. Each question is geared towards a type of knowledge that
meshes well when an organic, shared moment of intimacy.
