environment, roll+Cyberbrain. On a 10+, you do it and may choose two.
On a 7–9, you do it and may choose one.
 You take no Harm in the doing.
 The Veil does not change or respond as a result.

As this move implies, there isn’t a safe method of leaving the digital
environment, aside from the methods you come up with when first
creating the setting together. You and the other players will talk about
this, among other things, in depth on your first session together. There is
more information about the first session on page 271.
At the start of their first session, the players decided that there’s a set
time in the real world, and once that time is up, this ejection automatically
takes place, sending them out of the digital space. That means they have
a set time to accomplish this mission.
