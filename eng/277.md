In the same vein, cybernetics can be brought up as often as you like. You
have the fictional positioning when you have cybernetics, along with the
negative Tags that could come into play alongside it. Invoking either will
be contributing to the story. The fact that cybernetics are in the setting
means, like Obligation and Cred, you can play them up and use them
very lightly, or as deeply as you like—creating social and cultural aspects
to explore on your setting playbook that engage directly with how much
they have affected culture. Maybe just having them there is enough to
get the tone of your vision of the future into your own setting, too. You
are encouraged to give yourself exactly the kind of cyberware you want,
do not be dissuaded because of negative Tags. You first need to fail in
order for them to become relevant beyond the implications that they exist.
Remember you can modify them in game with the Plasma And Chrome
move, as well. This is another thing you can interact with as much or
as little as you like, creating a flexible system in which to embody your
cyberpunk fiction, unique to you and the table.
Technologies within The Veil should fit the setting. The Tag system is
nebulous and accommodating to the inventive and imaginative. Is
technology a good thing or a bad thing in the future? What will it do—
could it do? Don’t be afraid to break from the example Tags and really get
inventive with creating your own tech, distinctive to the setting.
Never be afraid to have conversations with the MC and other players about
what things you like and what things you do not—using the things you like
the most to bring about and express the tone and feel that you personally
would like the most.
Don’t forget to be a punk.
Remember: at it’s core, all you need is the stratification of class, an
emphasis on technologies beyond us (presently), and characters who
interact with the system. Either embodying it, personifying it, or resisting
it. Resistance takes many forms, as does the oppression of people. Do
not feel confined to the things you have consumed.
Within this sub genre of science fiction there are many cyberpunk stories
as yet unimagined.
