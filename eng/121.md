THE APPARATUS is shrouded in questions. It
doesn’t know how it came into the world and
does not have a clear picture of humanity.
As a newly awakened life form, it struggles
to understand its own emotions. Possessing
an artiﬁcial intelligence, it needs to acquire
more information to find its place in the
world and unravel the mystery of its existence — were they built to uplift, emulate or
destroy humanity?
