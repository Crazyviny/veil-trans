you, called Essence. It is assumed all of your people can use Essence for
their various own purposes. Though, if what they do is similar or not to you
is up to you and the MC to define, they still damage the Homeland —just
as you do when trigger moves that consume Essence. Using Essence
always harms your Homeland in the same way. It is by design that you’ll
play to find out and define yourself why people use Essence, which again,
causes the Homeland to be damaged. Work with the MC to answer these
questions, if you decide that your protagonist knows of this. As always
do what you think would be the most interesting for the fiction. Perhaps
your people don’t know that what they are doing with Essence damages
the Homeland, attributing the damage to something else entirely. Or
perhaps some don’t care, or they believe the gain from doing so makes
it worthwhile, regardless.

interesting choice for you as well as what will fit your fiction, depending
on what’s already been established.
