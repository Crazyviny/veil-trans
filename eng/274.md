ADVANCING BASIC MOVES
When you choose to advance a move, there’s a new option when you hit
a 12+ for it. Otherwise it remains the same. The more improvements you
take where you increase your State modifier, the more likely it is you will
roll higher for each. Mechanically, the only way to get something out of
this increase is to use an improvement to get an advanced basic move.
You’ll use basic moves most often when playing. Working toward increasing
your State’s will reflect character growth in the fiction because an increase
in a State is an increase in your protagonist being able to handle that
emotion better. Or at least depict a change in them. When you are able
to roll higher, consider unlocking a more substantial effect for those rolls.
