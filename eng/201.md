F F HARVEY TIME
When you have time, solitude, and offer someone Cred in the pursuit of
completing any contract you don’t intend to pursue yourself, roll+Cred
spent. On a 10+, all 4. On a 7–9, choose 2. On a miss, choose 1 and the
MC considers this a Golden Opportunity.
 They complete the contract.
 The board does not hear you have been pursuing your own self
interests.
 You get paid in full for the completion of the contract.
 The contract isn’t a setup or an ambush.
When you want to procure someone to do these contracts, they need to
actually be able to do these things for you. Simply assigning them the task
does not mean they are suited to it in the fiction. If they are a hit man and
you want them to do some nefarious Cred laundering, chances are they
won’t be able to help you with fulfilling that.
There is also a risk when doing this, if they do not succeed and it’s a
mandatory contract – there could be some pretty steep consequences that
may be hard to dig yourself out of. If the board heard about side contracts
being fulfilled but you not coming through on your mandatory ones...Why,
who knows what they’d do.
This move abstracts the entire contract you give to someone else, keeping
in mind that the assumption is that an NPC is assigned the contract. When
and if a PC were to take a job, this move would not apply. Only if you are
not involved and pursuing this yourself can you use this move, intended
to quickly resolve matters off screen when and if you choose to pursue
paying contracts or take the chance of assigning mandatory contracts
and divesting yourself from the outcomes. The MC can have this play out
in the fiction in a multitude of ways, all of which they have purview over in
order to meet the requirements of your choices. Balancing between all of
these options when you can’t choose them all, including not having the
contract be a setup or ambush, is all part of the precarious balancing act
you need to do as the Executive.
