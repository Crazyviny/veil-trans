THE HONED
The Honed should be the best at physical stuff, no matter the
circumstances. Chances are good the player who chooses the Honed is
attracted to the idea of a simple and low cognitive load playbook. Anytime
there are sports and physical exertion, the limits of the human body
questions, the MC should not be filling in these blanks, the Honed should
be. They seek excellence, glory, and approval. They probably therefor, look
down on those who do not aspire to be greater than they are now.
Don’t miss the opportunity to have them flesh out the why of
their character. With a direct goal and theme should come more
characterization. If in the fiction, this character is isolated (apart from The
Veil) and only seen doing physical tasks, it is easy to over look why they do
what they do and why they may want approval. They should be the human
angle directly giving more context to tech and cybernetics. A different lens.
 Insert an NPC into their life that is the opposite of them. Make them
likable, test the natural beliefs that are implied in the playbook
through this person—whether they’ve made it a Belief or not.
 Give them people to save. Despite tech (maybe because of tech?)
Normal people are weak. The Honed needs Humanity to do their
cool stuff so they need to be able to do their tasks.
 Make sure their views on cybernetics are known and push there.
Be it via the state of the world, orchestrating conversations on the
matter between PCs or by injecting an NPC into the fiction for that
specific purpose, etc.
 If they look down on someone, especially with cybernetics, find out
why and interrogate it. Is it a social aspect to explore?
 Show them human weakness on a personal level, then incorporate
that weakness into the world.
