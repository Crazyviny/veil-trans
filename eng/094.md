Jams are also a useful tool to tie protagonist’s lives together, since
everyone most likely has a lifestyle in mind after they’ve established what
they do and how much Cred they get. You may know each other based on
your lifestyle, and perhaps, jams overlapping.
Ultimately everyone at the table needs to clarify the following
in terms of Cred:
 Write down their jam.
 Establish your lifestyle based on your jam.
 Explore if these jams are worth spotlighting on screen and how

economy within your fiction and what pace the story is going
at, per session.
 Tie your protagonists with the others as loosely or as closely as you
