F F GLORY
Whenever you complete any of the feats below, you generate 1
humanity for each task:
 Defeat a worthy foe.
 Extract a VIP (your call) and see them to safety.
 Obtain an item of special importance.
 Go to and return from an infamously dangerous location.
 Liberate something of importance.
Set free someone, or something, wrongfully imprisoned.
Glory could be taken by other classes in order to generate humanity but
is the most suited towards the Honed interests. By rising above others
and doing difficult to impossible tasks, you are rewarded with humanity.
