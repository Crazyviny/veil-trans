MOVES

While other playbooks must lift the veil to pierce the
mixed reality in order to see what is the physical,
you do not. You have no neurochip and have a
consciousness unpaired with the technology that
everyone else is constantly connected to. You will
always see things without the added beneﬁt of tech
which will be a detriment and an advantage. You
will always be apart from “them”, apart from the
world and society, but you will also be the only one
with the ability to see the world for what it truly is
and in what ways this tech is impacting humanity
as you know it.

When you ad people out of danger, name up to
three people you wish to save. When you save all of
them you generate 2 humanity. When you try and
fail to save anyone you name, generate 1 humanity.

You get the Potential move and can choose one more.
