THE PRINCIPLES
The Agenda most resembles your goals. What you’re striving for and trying
to accomplish. The Principles are the heart of the game; and its soul.
The Agenda are the bones (all of which can be deemed essential to a
body of fiction to function and behave properly). Both working in unison
is what you need to have a “healthy” body of fiction. The heart though, is
most important.
The Principles can be interpreted as rules as well, they are more likely
to be taken as best practices to produce the most exceptional version
of the fiction you can. When you do these things, you’ll find the system
working with you. If you don’t, the game is probably going to feel strange.
The “good” parts of it will come in fits and starts, instead of an even flow
throughout. The “starts” will be when you are taking into consideration
your Principles and Agenda, the “fits” will be when you’re not doing those
things, with the result being a break in the fiction and possibly, tone.
 Color as much of the world as you can.
 Always be working toward the exploration of the Final Question.
 Push their Beliefs.
 Make their feelings as important as Harm.
 Address the protagonists not the players.
 Interweave the protagonist’s stories,
even across distance and boundaries.
 Put them at the center of everything.
 The fiction makes your moves, not you.
 Name everyone and give them a Motivation.
 Treat them as they were meant to be treated.
 Ask questions all of the time and answer them within the fiction.
 Be a fan of the protagonists.
 Give them a chance to think.
 Spotlight everybody.
 Make things costly and destructible.
COLOR AS MUCH OF THE WORLD AS YOU CAN
All of it. Especially the little bits you picture in your head like what it smells
like around the corner or motion of a bobble-head swinging from side to
side on someone’s futuristic flying car. Whenever you’re framing a scene,
the protagonists should feel like they’re there. They taste the smog filtering
in from the vents, they sniff the cigarettes another passenger is smoking.
