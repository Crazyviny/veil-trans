 A place to fill in a protagonist’s cybernetics (**and how they got
them**), if **applicable**.
The place to fill in cybernetics is there for the player to detail any cybernetic
enhancements they want their protagonist to start with, **as well as** how
they got them. Depending on what **you choose, you** are able to detail that
**particular cybernetic enhancement** by assigning it a number of Tags equal
to the number in the brackets beside it. Finally, **remember that** any piece
of cybernetics must also be assigned a negative Tag.
 A section that details a number of moves a protagonist both starts
out with and may acquire through improvement.
The section that details the **playbook** moves a protagonist has can be
found on the right-hand side. Beside each move there is a **square**. If **it** is
already marked in by default, it means that **you begin** play with that move,
which is aptly referred to as a starting move. There are moves listed below
it and those that have empty **boxes** are acquired later on in play, when **you
gain enough XP to select one as** a **reward.
 A Space detailing the playbook’s special move (simply referred** to
**as the playbooks “special”).
In the special section there is a specific move for every playbook. When
protagonists share a moment of intimacy with someone, be it physical or
emotional, these moves are triggered. In The Veil, intimacy is important**
and **underlined with specific moves showcasing the intention that
interpersonal play be touched upon. Something so important in the
protagonists lives as these intense and intimate moments have serious
implications, which then require these special moves. They are all different
and are geared towards each specific playbook.
