F F ARCHITECT SPECIAL
When the Architect shares a moment of intimacy with someone, be it
physical or emotional, roll without adding a State. On a hit, all is well. On
a miss, they are added to your subconscious as a named NPC. The MC
will ask you questions about your perception of them; answer truthfully.
**Just as the subconscious choices impact the fiction a lot, adding another
person should as well. After all, it is your perception of this person that
gets added to the subconscious, not the actual person**.
