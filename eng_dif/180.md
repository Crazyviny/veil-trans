The disease that is destroying you graces you with an additional benefit
before the end. Pick an additional option from The Gift **section**.
When choosing another **option from The** Gift **section**, make sure
you introduce it into the story too. It’s always fun when you get a new
advancement but if it’s suddenly just there, it probably won’t make much
sense. It doesn’t have to be something super detailed. **Remember, new
moves give you more fictional positioning, which should be explained
within** the **fiction**.
AMY: Looks like a good opportunity to check out my new

gift from The Grace move. My organic tissue regrows,
these days; I may just need it if this all goes sideways!
I’m going to activate our defense systems, which is
like a rail gun or turret thing, right? Soon as that’s
done, I zoom over there as fast as I can. **I don’t think
I know about this gift yet either, I’ll discover it when it
comes up, I think**.
MC: You’re super-fast **and come into the room. I think** you go

into something like bullet time, **you know**? As you see
the turrets tracking his movement, pumping out shells
into **the** furniture just a half second short of where
they’re running. **The room is getting Messed Up as
this person runs for their life. They** must have on some
sort of camouflage too, because as you’re running, you
see only feet and a head running along. You **do** see
a pink Mohawk, unwavering even while running and
maybe pale skin. I think that’s about all you can make
out from your vantage point. And as you’re running you
feel the Trigger coming on for your disease. I think it
only happens after you finish using your gift though. So,
when you stop running and using your speed **is when
you’ll probably Trigger, and then** who knows **what will
happen**! What do you do?
