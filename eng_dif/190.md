F F CHARGE OBJECT
When you channel the energy you have from your Flow and use it to
charge an object, roll and add +1 for each Flow you **choose to consume**.
On a hit, when **someone** touches **the** object **the charge dissipates
and** you choose 1:
 They suffer Humanity Harm **by way of one of your own memories**.
 You **glean** how ______ relates to **them**.
 You learn what emotion they are feeling, **as well as** any thoughts
or memories tied to **them**.
 Hold 1; spend **the** Hold **1 for 1 in order** to **Help** or **Hinder** them
despite any and all barriers that would normally prevent it,
including distance.
On a 7–9, **you also ingrain a part of yourself within the charge that you
did not intend to: a memory, an emotion**, or **a secret**. The **target chooses
which**.
The Empath can **select how much** Flow **they use in order** to charge an
object. You can divine a relationship between **themselves and** something
they are aware of when they touch the object, inflict Humanity Harm, or
feel the strongest emotion they feel **right now**, and for whom. **Helping** or
**Hindering someone** from anywhere **will usually just be used for another
protagonist, but not always. On a 7-9 result, the risk of the move comes
into play, as you leave a part of yourself there for them to experience** when
they touch **the object; as well as your actual intentions. As such, you may
want to use as much Flow as you have spare to get the desired result,
depending on the situation. The charge is only good for one touch, will you
risk imprinting a piece of yourself within** it?
